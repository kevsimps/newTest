# Lab 1: Getting started with Collaboration Control Hub

In this lab, you’ll log in with your instructor provided credentials and update the timeout settings.

### Log into Control Hub: [admin.webex.com](http://admin.webex.com/)

* Use an incognito/private window in your browser
* Instructor provided log in details

### Management > Location > dCloud > PSTN

* Review info about the PSTN connection. Do not make any changes.

### Management > Location > dCloud > Calling

* Find voice portal settings. Do not make any changes.

### Management > Organization Settings > Control Hub Idle Timeout

* Change the timeout to 12 hours

### Services > Contact Center > Overview > Quick Links

* Verify access to Webex Connect and Webex Engage by clicking into each one. If you are unable to access any of them or don’t see the links, let your instructor know.
