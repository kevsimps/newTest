# Lab 3: Tenant settings

In this lab, you will learn how to synchronize Webex users to Contact Center and change tenant desktop settings.

### After updating Webex profiles in Control Hub, syncing them with Webex Contact Center is required.

Contact Center > Tenant Settings > General

* Click to synchronize users

### When calls are completed a wrap-up code should automatically be assigned after 60 seconds.

Contact Center > Tenant Settings > Desktop

* Auto Wrapup Interval: 60 seconds
